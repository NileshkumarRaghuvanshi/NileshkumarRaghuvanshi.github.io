About

Embedded Linux engineer with 14 years in kernel drivers, board bring-up and BSP work, the last 5 on camera and vision pipelines for an edge-AI SoC. At SiMa.ai I led board bring-up of the Modalix SoC in U-Boot (DDR training, secure boot), led first-silicon bring-up of the ARM Mali-C71AE ISP and Synopsys MIPI CSI-2 receiver, and brought up 4 image sensors (Sony IMX477, IMX568, onsemi AR0234, OmniVision OG05C10) through V4L2 to GStreamer ML pipelines. Earlier work spans Linux network drivers and L2/L3 multicast at Cisco, I2C and power-management firmware at HPE, and automotive infotainment at Harman.
