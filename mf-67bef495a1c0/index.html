How this list is made. From the latest month of fund portfolios, using the rules the backtest supported: the 15 stocks with the most funds net buying (buyers minus sellers), plus any stock that 2 or more fund houses bought for the first time. A Candidate is also in a price uptrend (above its 200-day average and higher than 3 months ago) and above its 50-day average. Caution: a mid cap the funds have been buying 3+ months running, which did worse in the backtest. Check: listed too recently to test the trend. Wait: not in an uptrend yet, or below its 50-day average.

The backtest's edge came from holding several of these together, not from any single pick, and from selling on price (a 15% to 20% trailing stop did best). These are candidates from this analysis for your own research, not investment advice.

Checked after each NSE close. A stock that closes below its 50-day average is named in the project thread so you can decide whether to sell.
